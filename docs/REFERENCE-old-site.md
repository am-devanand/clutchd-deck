## How it works
- **Lede:** "From request to completed service, see how ClutchD connects you with verified
  providers in minutes."
- **"From breakdown to back on the road"** — six real job states:
  1. Request — share the problem and your location.
  2. Match — finds verified nearby providers. (status: Searching)
  3. Accept — a mechanic takes the job. (Accepted)
  4. En route — track arrival with live ETA. (En route)
  5. In progress — approve the estimate as the work happens. (In progress)
  6. Done — pay securely, review, and the service joins your vehicle's digital history. (Completed)
- **THE ECOSYSTEM — "Every service, connected"** — "puts drivers, mechanics, garages, fleets,
  parts, payments and service history on one system, so the vehicle's story never starts over."
  Nodes: Driver · Vehicle · Service Request · Mechanic · Garage · Parts · Payment · Service
  History · Vehicle Intelligence.
- **INTELLIGENCE — "Maintenance that happens before things break"** — reactive → proactive:
  - Maintenance reminders — records turn into future needs; told before it's a problem.
  - Vehicle health insights — live view of systems, battery to brakes.
  - Predictive maintenance & early warnings — anomalies flag issues early.
  - Fleet intelligence — proactive scheduling replaces reactive firefighting.
  - Health preview: Battery 78% healthy · Brake wear 62% due soon · Next service in 2 months.
